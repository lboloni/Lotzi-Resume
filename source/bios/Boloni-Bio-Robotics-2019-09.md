### **Synergistic activities**

### 

### **Development of research tools:**

- The YAES simulation framework (http://www.cs.ucf.edu/~lboloni/Programming/Yaes/) takes the agent perspective to the simulation of communicating embodied agents such as mobile sensor networks or mobile robots. YAES was used in more than 40 publications and several PhD dissertations, including a best paper award.

- The Xapagy cognitive architecture aims to model and mimic the way humans reason about stories ([<u>http://www.xapagy.org</u>](http://www.xapagy.org/)). A paper describing its functionality received the Kurzweil Best AGI Idea Prize 2014

**Development of computation methodologies, and algorithms for problem-solving:**

- In the field of meta-program scheduling, he introduced the notion of *robust scheduling*, an application scheduling approach which takes into account the likelihood of appearance of hazards during computation and develops schedules which are less likely to be affected by these hazards (1999-2003, with D.C. Marinescu). He participated in a comparative study of task allocation methodologies which is now considered one of the reference texts of the topic (1860 citations according to Google Scholar) (1999-2005, the effort was led by H.J. Siegel of Colorado State).

### **Service to the scientific and engineering community:**

- Had extensively participated in the open source community, in particular in the KDE desktop environment where he contributed to applications such as konsole, korganizer and gofai-wmt, and wrote a book on KDE programming. He also participated in the work of the Open Document Foundation, his group developing an extensive test-suite for the OpenDocument document standard.

### **Innovations in teaching and training:** 

- Developed two courses at the Dept of EECS at UCF (an undergraduate elective course “Multi Agent Systems” and an advanced graduate level course “Engineering Applications of Autonomous Agents”. He developed a special topic course on "Sensor Networks - a Multi Agent Perspective" and offered it in Spring 2008.
