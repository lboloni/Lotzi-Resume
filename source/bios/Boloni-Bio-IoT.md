### **Synergistic activities**

### 

### **Development of research tools:**

- Developed the YAES simulation framework (http://www.cs.ucf.edu/~lboloni/Programming/Yaes/) that takes the agent perspective to the simulation of communicating embodied agents such as mobile sensor networks or mobile robots. YAES was used in more than 40 publications and several PhD dissertations, including a best paper award.

- Developed the Xapagy cognitive architecture which aims to model and mimic the way humans reason about stories ([<u>http://www.xapagy.org</u>](http://www.xapagy.org/)). A paper describing its functionality received the Kurzweil Best AGI Idea Prize 2014.

**Development of computation methodologies, and algorithms for problem-solving:**

- Introduced the notion of *robust scheduling*, an application scheduling approach which takes into account the likelihood of appearance of hazards during computation and develops schedules which are less likely to be affected by these hazards.

### **Service to the scientific and engineering community:**

- Extensively contributed to the open source community, in particular to the KDE desktop environment.

### **Innovations in teaching and training:** 

- Developed an advanced graduate level course “Engineering Applications of Autonomous Agents” at the Dept of CS at UCF.
